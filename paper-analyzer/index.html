Accurate Structure Prediction of Biomolecular Interactions with AlphaFold 3

Josh Abramson, Jonas Adler, Jack Dunger, Richard Evans, Tim Green, Alexander Pritzel, Olaf Ronneberger, et al.

AlphaFold 3 introduces a unified diffusion-based architecture that jointly predicts 3D structures of complexes across all biomolecular classes—proteins, DNA, RNA, ligands, ions, and chemical modifications—with atomic accuracy, surpassing specialized docking and folding tools.
